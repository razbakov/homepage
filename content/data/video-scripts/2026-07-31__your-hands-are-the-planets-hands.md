# Daily — Your Hands Are the Planet's Hands

**Source:** https://razbakov.com/blog/2026-07-30-your-hands-are-the-planets-hands

## Hook (0:00–0:20)

We keep asking the wrong question about consciousness. We ask *where does it come from* — as if it's a thing you could find if you just looked harder. But you can't find the one who's looking by looking. Today I want to flip the question entirely.

`[B-ROLL: Close-up of hands on a desk, slowly opening palms upward]`
`[TEXT ON SCREEN: "You can't find the one who's looking."]`

## Point 1 — The Brain Is a Window (0:20–1:10)

Here's the first move, and everything hangs on it. The brain does not produce consciousness. It localises it. Think of an eye. An eye doesn't make light — it gathers it, bends it, gives it a point of entry. But the light was already there before the eye opened. I think the brain stands in the same relation to consciousness. Not a factory. A window.

And if that's true — if your mind is not manufacturing awareness but *focusing* it — then the local mind is a point in something much wider. The model I'm building draws it as a nesting chain: absolute consciousness at the top, then galaxy, solar system, Earth, and then you. Your attention is the finest tip of something that runs all the way up. You are the instrument through which the planet can finally know, decide, and act.

`[B-ROLL: Time-lapse of clouds forming over Earth from orbit, slowly zooming in toward a city]`
`[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Not a factory. A window."]`

## Point 2 — Where the Regress Stops (1:10–2:10)

Now, every model like this runs into the same trap. If consciousness is being steered, who steers it? Put a controller above the mind — a soul, a god of the machine — and you've solved nothing. Because now you have to ask who controls the controller. It never ends.

There's one way out, and it's the sharpest idea in the whole essay. Stop treating consciousness as an object that needs an operator. Choosing is not something consciousness *does*. Choosing is what consciousness *is*. Strip away thought, emotion, memory — what's left is one irreducible thing: the capacity to direct attention. To select. That's the floor. The chain doesn't need a driver, because the driving is the thing itself.

And once choice is the centre, four words cover everything. Consciousness is awareness. Spirit is direction. Soul is the memory of experience. Body is action. One sentence: the universe knows itself through consciousness, feels itself through the soul, and changes itself through the body. You're all three at once.

`[B-ROLL: A prism splitting white light into a spectrum, then reversing]`
`[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Choosing is what consciousness IS."]`

## Point 3 — The Steady Hand (2:10–3:05)

Here's where it gets personal. If all of that is true — if your hands are functionally the planet's hands — then steadiness matters. Most of a human life is spent being pushed along the emotional spectrum. A feeling arrives, the body reacts before the mind has chosen anything. That's not freedom. That's weather moving through you.

Evolution, in this model, isn't humanity pulling away from nature. It's the opposite — more and more conscious participation in the life of the planet. And enlightenment? It's not a glow. Not an escape. It's the moment your choice becomes fully conscious — the moment it stops being decided for you by automatic reaction. The hand stops twitching to the weather and begins to move on purpose.
