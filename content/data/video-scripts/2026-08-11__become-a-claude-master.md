## Point 1 — One command, three phases (0:20–1:10)

Here's how it works. You type one slash command — `/become-claude-master` — and it walks you through three phases. First, it sets up your foundation. There's a file called CLAUDE.md that Claude reads in every conversation. Think of it as your personal instruction manual for the AI. The skill creates it, seeds it with five starter rules — things like "say 'rule' and Claude saves it," "say 'learned' and it extracts lessons from what just happened." These rules compound. After a week, Claude genuinely understands how you work. Five minutes and you've got a foundation most users never build.

## Point 2 — It interviews you (1:10–2:10)

Phase two is where it gets interesting. The skill interviews you. Not a generic form — it asks what you actually do. Your stack, your tools, whether you write content or manage projects or review pull requests. Then it recommends skills from a curated collection and installs them. A frontend dev gets test-driven development and PR review tools. A content creator gets social posting and viral threads. A project manager gets workflow and estimation. You don't browse a catalog. The tool matches itself to your work. And phase three — there's a full 16-topic curriculum. Prompting patterns, debugging strategies, multi-agent workflows, the memory system. Each session is about fifteen minutes. Claude fetches the latest docs, gives you a mini-challenge, records your score. It's structured learning built into the tool you're already using.
